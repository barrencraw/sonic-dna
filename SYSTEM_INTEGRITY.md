# System Integrity Mechanic

## Tradeoff: Operational Capacity vs. Complexity
The system hardware degrades under the load of complex DNA processing.

- **What the player wants**: To process high-complexity DNA (high layering/variation) for superior audio generation.
- **What the world demands**: The hardware has finite "Integrity" that degrades under heavy load.
- **What gets worse**:
    - Processing High-Complexity DNA (TEX layering > 500 or variation > 7000) drains Integrity significantly (-10 units).
    - Standard processing drains Integrity slightly (-2 units).
    - At 0 Integrity, the system refuses all non-maintenance tasks.
- **Skillful play**: Interspersing high-scarcity "Maintenance" DNA (TEX scarcity > 9000) to restore Integrity (+5 units) without stalling production.

## Values
- Starting Integrity: 100
- Min Integrity: 0
- Max Integrity: 100
- High Complexity Drain: 10
- Standard Drain: 2
- Maintenance Restore: 5
